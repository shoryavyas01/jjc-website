OUR BEGINNING / 1993

Local roots.
A practical outlook.

Infrastructure begins with understanding the ground beneath it.

Jai Jwala Contracts is based in Pokaran in Rajasthan’s Jaisalmer district. Our work spans civil construction, bitumen and concrete roads, and the supply of construction aggregates.

Through Jai Jwala Stone Crusher and our contracting work, we connect the materials needed for a project with the work that brings it to life.
